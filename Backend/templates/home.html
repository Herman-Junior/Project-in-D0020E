Database Management System

This application allows you to manage sensor, weather, and audio data for your LTU lawn mowers.

1. Insert Data

Upload CSV files containing sensor or weather data, or add audio recordings directly from the Insert page. Files are processed and stored automatically.

2. Lookup & Download Data

Browse and filter sensor, weather, and combined data by date and time on the Lookup page. Select specific rows to download, or download everything matching your current filters as a CSV file.

3. Safe Deletion

Deleting a file gives you the option to restore it for up to one week. Deleted items are held in the Trash Can where you can review and recover them before they are permanently removed.
